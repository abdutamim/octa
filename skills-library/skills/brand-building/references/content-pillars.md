# Content Pillars & Content Plan (2025)

Content Pillars are not just topics — they're the **strategic identity** the brand stands on.
Without them you talk about everything and no one understands who you are. With them, every
piece of content serves a clear direction and cements your identity in the audience's mind.
Use this whenever someone wants a content plan, a content calendar, or their pillars defined.

Distinguish two layers:
- **Content Pillars** = *what* you talk about (the topics).
- **Content Strategy** = *how / when / to whom* — the plan that makes those topics work toward a
  goal.

There are **two complementary frameworks** below. Use the **5 Pillars** to balance content
*types*; use the **3C Funnel** to map content to the *buyer journey*. For a full plan, combine
them (a pillar can be tagged by which funnel stage it serves). The Excel asset
`assets/content-pillars-3C-template.xlsx` implements the 3C model and is ready to fill.

---

## Framework A — The 5 Content Pillars

1. **Educational Value** — build trust, prove you're useful.
   Real solutions to real problems, practical steps. Makes people remember your name.
   *Ex: fitness tips, before/after decor.*

2. **Authority & Proof** — prove you don't just talk, you deliver.
   Success stories, results, personal experiences. This is what makes people pay more.
   *Ex: growth-formula videos, client stories from an agency.*

3. **Personal & Relatable** — build a real human relationship.
   Life moments, challenges, daily life, behind-the-scenes. Makes people say "that's me!"
   *Ex: a video about jealousy, a day at the office with the team.*

4. **Conversion Content** — turn followers into clients *without looking salesy*.
   Smart CTA, answer objections, offers/solutions.
   *Ex: a freelancer ad, a coach explaining their service's value realistically.*

5. **Trend-Adaptive** — reach new audiences and prove you're current.
   Merge a trend with your niche, or a smart reaction to a viral event. High reach — but it must
   *serve* the brand, not dilute it.
   *Ex: a "Bugatti Arabic"-style video, a smart trend campaign.*

**Applying it:**
1. Choose **3–5 fixed pillars** matching the person and niche.
2. Build an **Idea Bank** per pillar (e.g. 20 educational ideas, 10 personal stories…).
3. Organize on Notion / Google Sheet.
4. Weekly, review: is the content **balanced** across types?

Balance check questions to ask a user: Are the last 5 videos balanced across Value / Personal /
Proof? How many of the last 5 actually served a strategic goal?

## Framework B — The 3C Funnel (the Excel template)

Maps content to where the audience is in the buying journey. Each stage has sub-pillars:

**PILLAR 1 — Connecting** (top of funnel; attract cold audience)
- *Creative Disruptive Content* — pattern-breaking, unexpected framing.
- *Problem Aware Content* — name the pain the audience feels.
- *Trend Integrated Niche Content* — trends tied back to your niche.

**PILLAR 2 — Consideration** (middle; build trust & authority)
- *Educational Frameworks* — named methods, principles, models.
- *How-To Tutorials* — step-by-step, tools, quick wins.
- *Process Content* — behind-the-scenes of how you think/work.

**PILLAR 3 — Closing** (bottom; convert)
- *Before & After Content* — transformation/results.
- *Testimonials* — client voices, DMs, proof.
- *Hard Selling* — explicit offer + clear CTA + urgency.

Each row in the template has: **Sub-pillar | IDEAS (samples) | YOUR IDEAS | CONTENT TYPE |
CHANNEL(S)**. The template ships with real Arabic example ideas per sub-pillar the user can adapt.

## Producing a content plan

**Intake:** niche, audience, the offer/what they sell, main platform(s), posting frequency,
primary goal (reach vs. trust vs. sales), and their voice/angle.

**Then produce (adapt to what was asked and to the user's language):**
1. **3–5 chosen pillars** (from Framework A) tailored to them, each tagged with the funnel stage
   it serves (from Framework B).
2. An **Idea Bank** — concrete, niche-specific ideas per pillar (aim for ~15–30 total across
   pillars for a starter bank). Ground each idea in a real problem/angle, not generic filler.
3. A **weekly cadence** balancing the pillars/stages (e.g. a 7-post week mixing Value, Personal,
   Proof, one Conversion, one Trend).
4. Optionally a **status workflow** for each idea: Scripted → Ready → Filmed → Posted.

**Output mode:**
- *In-chat plan* → present pillars + idea bank + cadence cleanly.
- *File requested* → fill the Excel template (`content-pillars-3C-template.xlsx`) with their
  ideas in the "YOUR IDEAS" column, or build a fresh sheet/doc. Follow the `xlsx` skill for
  Excel output and the `docx` skill for a Word plan.

**Completion prompt:** "When someone sees my content, they must feel that I'm the person who ___."
