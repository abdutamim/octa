# 60-Day Plan — ردّاد launch (5 Aug → 3 Oct 2026)

Built for **25+ hrs/week**, zero ad budget in month 1. Read with
`tamim-raddad.md` (positioning) and `tamim-market-map.md` (evidence).

**Goal for 60 days:** 2 paid pilots + 1 core client (≈ AED 6,500 + 2,400/mo),
and one public demo asset that sells while asleep.
Not a follower target. Followers are a by-product here.

---

## The three tracks (run in parallel, never merge them)

| Track | Job | Hrs/wk | Success in 60 days |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — ردّاد** (front door) | Real estate WhatsApp + store brain. The brand and the recurring revenue | 12 build | 1 core client signed |
| **B — n8n / automation gigs** (cash now) | Live freelance demand, no language moat needed. Pays while A compounds | 5 | 2 gigs closed |
| **C — content + outreach** (the engine) | 7 posts/wk + 10 real DMs/day | 6 + 2 retro | 30 qualified conversations |

**Rule:** Track B money never becomes the identity. It funds Track A. If a B
client asks for a site, quote the integrated build (Track A pricing).

---

## Weekly hour split

```
Build & deliver   12h   (Track A demo/clients + Track B gigs)
Content            6h   (1 filming day → 3–5 reels · 1 design session → the week's carousels)
Outreach & sales   5h   (10 DMs/day, 5 days = 50/wk)
Retro & numbers    2h
```

## Content system

**Pillars (4):** `يبني` build/teach · `يثبت` proof/demo · `رأي` contrarian ·
`قصة` journey.

**Named series — «ردّ في ٦٠ ثانية»** — weekly, fixed format: one real Arabic
customer question, answered live on screen by the bot, in dialect, timer
visible. This is the reach engine and the proof engine at once.
⚠️ Never name or mock a real business's slow reply. Use own demo data or an
anonymised, permission-given example. (`13` red line.)

**Cadence:** 7 posts/week — 3 Connect · 3 Consider · 1 Close. Post 6–9pm Cairo.
**Anchor post every 2 weeks** (days 12, 26, 40, 54) — highest-effort piece.

**CTA keyword:** «ردّاد» in DMs.

# DAYS 31–60 — scale

**Honest note:** days 31–60 are deliberately planned at *week* level, not
day level. Ref `09` requires the month-1 retro to drive month 2 — pre-writing
56 fixed hooks would be false precision. The business milestones below are
fixed; the content branches on what weeks 1–4 actually proved.

## Branch rule (apply at day 30)

```
If ≥1 pilot sold  → double down: same vertical, same format, raise volume,
                    add the second vertical (car dealerships — same mechanic)
If 0 pilots but   → offer problem. Rebuild the offer doc, cut price scope not
   DMs are coming    price, add a stronger guarantee. Content unchanged.
If 0 DMs at all   → attention problem (`01`). Hooks and format, not substance.
                    Rebuild the first 2 seconds of every reel.
```

| Week | Dates | Build focus | Content focus | Milestone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **5** | 2–8 Sep | Convert pilot → core client. Package the build as a repeatable install | Winner posts reissued 5 ways. Series continues weekly | Core proposal sent |
| **6** | 9–15 Sep | **Vertical #2: car dealerships.** Same mechanic, swap vocabulary (stock, finance, service) | Same content, dealership examples. Anchor day 40: the integrated store+brain argument | 10 dealership DMs/day |
| **7** | 16–22 Sep | Salla/Zid research → one custom theme or app scoped for a Saudi merchant | Saudi-facing content, SAR pricing visible | First Saudi conversation |
| **8** | 23–29 Sep | Productize: the install becomes a fixed SOP so it ships in 7 days repeatably | Anchor day 54: the 60-day build-in-public retrospective, real numbers only | Core client signed |
| **9 (part)** | 30 Sep – 3 Oct | **Meta billing change lands 1 Oct** — usage caps live for every client | Ride the deadline honestly: what changes, what it costs, what to do | Zero client surprised by a bill |

## Metrics — read weekly, act monthly

| Stage | Metric | Month-1 target (assumption, not promise) |
|---|---|---|
| Connect | reach · % non-followers | 60%+ non-followers |
| Consider | saves · profile visits | saves > likes |
| Close | DMs · pilot bookings | 15 real conversations |
| Outreach | reply % per channel | track IG vs LinkedIn separately, kill the loser |
| Money | pilots sold · core signed | 2 pilots by day 45 |

Likes are not a metric. **Saves, shares, DMs.**

## Expectations, stated plainly

Average organic growth for a small business account is ~8%/month. Month 1 is
foundation, not an explosion. The 60-day win condition is **paid pilots and one
core client**, not follower count — if 500 people see the demo and 3 of them
are brokers with budget, that beats 50,000 wrong views.
