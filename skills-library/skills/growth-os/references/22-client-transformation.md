# 22 — Client Transformation

The paid client brand-transformation workflow: **audit → backend foundation →
identity → surface fixes → content plan + KPIs.** Run the stages in this order;
each stage's output is the next stage's input. Case running through this file:
a fitness coach selling skinny→built transformation coaching.

---

## Rule zero

A personal brand is nothing but **the associations people make with you.** Copy
another creator and the associations point at them — the brand stops being personal
and becomes a reflection of everyone else's. Dissecting what makes the brand *theirs*
is the whole job.

Second law: **packaging must match the content.** Positioning as "raw and
trustworthy" fails the moment the content doesn't live up to the narrative.
Inconsistency between packaging and content is itself the diagnosis.

---

## Stage 1 — Audit (touch nothing yet)

Screenshot every surface first. Diagnose before designing.

| Surface | Red flag | Why it fails |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube ratio | **400 videos / 600 subscribers** | volume is fine, packaging is broken — this ratio IS the diagnosis |
| Banner | generic, could belong to anyone | spreads no original message |
| Channel bio | wall of text | nobody reads it; kills curiosity instead of spiking it |
| First link | Calendly / 1:1 coaching | transparent sell; viewers reach links through videos → description, not the channel page |
| Thumbnails | inconsistent, weak | reflects directly in flat views |
| IG handle | underscores, filler | unreadable, untypeable |
| IG name | decorative emoji | unprofessional unless the emoji does a job |
| Profile photo | messy background, mirror selfie | environment breaks the claim the body makes |
| Stories | dead / empty | audience goes cold between posts |
| Pinned post | most-viewed video | pinned must navigate, not brag |

---

## Stage 2 — Backend foundation (most people skip it; it matters most)

Laying stones before the visible build. Two documents, drafted with Claude in
~5 minutes each, then reviewed with the client. Rule for AI use: **you supply the
idea, AI enhances it — never a free slate.** Pull every claim from the client's real
life and existing footage.

### ICP doc

| Field | Case answers (pattern to copy) |
|---|---|
| Who | males ≈ **the creator's own age** — you attract the age you are; a mismatch attracts nobody |
| Body / situation | naturally skinny ectomorph; students or just starting work; little free time; trained before; already semi-educated in the space |
| Feels | overlooked by mainstream fitness — too much confusing noise |
| Wants | get bigger, stop being "the skinny guy", confidence, **mentorship from someone who has actually been there** (not a naturally stocky guy who got shredded) |
| Pains | listed explicitly, one per line |
| Mindset profile | how they think about the problem, written out |

### Positioning doc

- **Tone of voice:** raw and unfiltered · warm and approachable · genuinely humble ·
  **no ego** — alpha-male posturing drives this audience away.
- **Lifestyle pillars — derive them from footage that already exists,** never invent:
  outdoors/nature · discipline (early mornings, training in the cold) · aesthetic
  lifestyle, low cortisol.
- **Content mix:** value + real moments + talking-head behind the build + day-to-day.
- **Aesthetic upgrades that raise trust:** better clothing and environment. Dressing
  well → confidence on camera → perceived trust → easier sell. Cheap fix, real lift.

## Stage 3 — Identity

### Color — pull the palette FROM the client's real environment

1. Screenshot existing footage. Identify the **3–4 colors that already dominate**
   (case: black, white, blue, orange/brown).
2. Never build a palette opposite to where the client actually films — it makes every
   edit harder and destroys the real goal. **Consistency beats color choice.** Any
   palette works if it is kept.
3. **Blue + orange is light's native pair:** opposites on the color wheel AND the two
   Kelvin poles — every light source leans warm (sun) or cold (sky, screens). Car
   headlights migrated yellow-orange → blue-white for exactly this reason, and cinema
   grades on this pair. That is why it works in personal-brand footage by default.
4. Black and white are **bases, not brand colors** — but pick a bias (case: black).
5. **Document hex codes for every shade** in the brand doc so the editor copy-pastes
   instead of eyeballing.

### Type

- Generate candidates with AI ("similar to Inter or Roboto" is a sufficient brief).
- Differentiate with **letter-spacing changes**, not new families.
- The "third font" = the same family at thin weight with different tracking.
- **One family at 2 weights + tracking variation beats two families.**
- One playful script (case: Covered Your Grace) for rare accents only.

## Stage 4 — Surface fixes

**YouTube banner:** wide landscape from the client's real environment, subject tiny
in the middle, name set above. Simple, raw, on-palette.

**Profile photo:** shot **by someone else** — it signals effort a mirror selfie never
can. Clean, even background; natural tones; good clothing. No shirtless proof needed:
fitted clothing is enough of a hint. Same rule as the banner — environment must match
the claim.

**Video description template:**

```
[What the video is about — 1 line]
[What happens in it — 1 line]          ← 2 lines MAX, ever

[Main link — your site] — one short CTA ("Want this handled? DM me here")

Instagram: …
TikTok: …
X: …                                    ← socials listed plainly, 1-2-3-4

#yourname                               ← exactly ONE hashtag: your name.
                                          Owns the search result for you.
```

**Channel homepage:** pin one **VSL / blueprint video** at the top. Everyone who
lands anywhere on the brand can be routed back to this one video and nurtured from
it into booking a call. Kill the 1:1-coaching first link — the VSL replaces it.

**Instagram handle:** no underscores, no emoji. Simplify by **dropping vowels or
abbreviating** until it is short and typeable (chrstphr-style). Repeat a letter
before you accept an underscore.

**Instagram bio:**

```
Building a body and an unbreakable mind
120 lb → 155                            ← concrete numbers beat adjectives
[one link]
```

Match units to the audience's demographic (lb for US). Emoji only as a deliberate
memorable gimmick — a bat emoji instead of the word "night" — never decoration.

**Highlights — exactly 4:** `me · thoughts · life · clients`
- **me** = the story of how they got here, told as a sequence — it functions as a
  second VSL.
- Fold daily-content highlights into **life**; fold testimonials into **clients**.
- Clean, simple, on-palette covers.

**Pinned post:** navigational and aesthetic — the first thing a new visitor clicks
must explain who this is. Never pin the most-viewed post just because it's most
viewed.

## Stage 5 — Content plan + KPI math (work backwards from money)

```
Goal: $10k/month  →  needs 5 new clients/month  →  posting plan below
```

| Channel | Volume | Format split |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube | **4 videos/month** (1/week; raw style allows more) | 1 informational + 1 mindset/discipline + 1 daily-format mixing both + 1 experimental (Q&A — or another informational: info videos build the channel's foundation) |
| IG stories | **daily** | keeps the audience warm between posts |
| IG story sequence | **1/week** | one structured narrative sequence |
| IG clips | as many as possible | cut from the YouTube videos, funneling IG → YouTube |

**Funnel:** IG clips → YouTube videos → VSL → booked call.

**Conversion math (state the assumptions, then distrust them):**
- ~**0.5%** conversion on VSL viewers — flagged as extremely high; do not count on it.
- ≈ **500–1,000** engaged viewers (2+ videos incl. the VSL) per client landed.
- Target ≈ **40,000 total views** to net ≈ **10,000 people** who watch more than one
  video — raw views dilute; most people watch only one.

## Deliverable structure (this is a paid service — package it)

1. **Audit doc** — annotated screenshots of every surface, red flags named
2. **Foundation doc** — ICP + positioning (tone, pillars, content mix)
3. **Identity** — palette with per-shade hex + type system
4. **Surface redesign** — banner, profile photo, description template, bio, handle,
   highlights, pinned post
5. **90-day content plan** — volumes and format splits per channel
6. **KPIs** — goal → clients → volume → conversion assumptions, written down

---

## Use with

- **01 — Diagnosis**: Stage 1 audit is the diagnosis pass; the ratio diagnostic
  (volume vs subscribers) feeds the bottleneck call.
- **03 — Persona / Awareness**: the ICP doc is the persona input; age-mirror rule
  constrains who the brand can credibly target.
- **04 — Brand Identity**: environment-derived palette and one-family type override
  generic palette picking when the client already has footage.
- **09 — 30-Day Plan**: Stage 5 volumes and the backwards KPI math slot directly
  into the plan's cadence and targets.
