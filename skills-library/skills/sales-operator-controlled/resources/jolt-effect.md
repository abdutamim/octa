## 9. Cross-book Comparison Questions

### SPIN Selling

- SPIN drives the customer to articulate their own needs via Implication/Need-payoff questions; JOLT urges *limiting* exploration and *offering* a recommendation. Is JOLT a *late-stage* counterpart applied after needs are clear, or a genuine conflict over how much the seller should tell vs. ask?

### Gap Selling

- Gap Selling expands the gap/pain to motivate change; JOLT warns that amplifying pain on an *indecisive* buyer backfires. How do we reconcile pain-widening (early) with risk-reduction (late)? Are they sequential rather than contradictory?

### The Challenger Sale

- Challenger prescribes *constructive tension* and *taking control*; JOLT prescribes a low-effort *"buyer's agent"* posture. Do tension and reassurance target different buyer states (status-quo vs. indecision)?

### Influence

- Cialdini champions *scarcity* and *urgency*; JOLT classifies these as FUD that backfires on indecisive buyers. When does scarcity help vs. harm, conditioned on buyer anxiety?

### Getting to Yes

- How does "take risk off the table" (opt-outs, phased deals) relate to principled negotiation's "invent options for mutual gain" and objective criteria?
