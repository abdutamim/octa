# Copy — the hook, the slides, the CTA

> The visual craft lives in the other files. This is the part that gets written.

---

## The chassis

**6–8 slides. One idea per slide.** Two ideas means two slides.

```text
1  cover        the hook — brakes the scroll
2  mirror       describes their situation exactly → "that's me"
3  objection    breaks what they are already thinking
4  mechanism    explains why it happens → this is what builds authority
5  grid         4–6 points they can act on now → this is what gets saved
6  proof        real work / a principle / an attributed number   (optional)
7  offer        what they get — at zero risk
8  CTA          one word
```

**Tension-to-release ratio ≈ 2:1.** Six slides of tension to three of
resolution. The strongest reference spent **6 slides** building the problem
before it handed over the first fix.

**The naked-promise slide:** between the problem and the solution, spend a
**whole slide** on the promise and nothing else — enormous type, no image, no
body copy. That slide is what says "that chapter is closed, the new one starts
here."

## The hook

**People don't read, they scan.** A hook is not a headline. It is a brake.

| Mechanism | How | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Curiosity gap** | starts an answer and doesn't finish it | the default; works on any topic |
| **Reframe** | kills a belief they hold as basic | "a carousel isn't read, it's scanned" |
| **Counter-expectation** | breaks a widely held belief | the audience already tried the obvious fix and it failed |
| **Loss** | shows them they are paying right now | there is real money on the table — a SaaS team billing for seats nobody logs into |
| **Mirror** | describes their exact situation → "that's me" | cold audience — the strongest one for saves |
| **Number** | a specific that stops the eye | you have a real number with a source |
| **Mechanism** | promises to expose the why | authority posts |
| **Confession about yourself** | "you'll notice we stopped doing X" | stronger than generic advice — because it is checkable |
| **Borrowed authority** | "the platform's own team said it" | your credibility is still being built |
| **Question aimed at their competence** | "can a stranger find your fees in one tap?" | puts the reader on the defensive |

### The rules
- **6 words or fewer** per line.
- **One punch word** carries the accent. One.
- The test: **does it stop the scroll without a caption explaining it?** The
  caption is a crutch — take it away.
- **Close on "…" or "?"** so it pulls into the next slide.

> ⚠️ **A typeset hook is not a spoken opener.** Any "opening line" lifted from a
> video script arrives too long. The cover is sized for **6 words**, with the
> hero word at **74–79% of the canvas width**. Do not drop a reel's opening line
> onto a cover unchanged — it will overflow.

### Filtering by kill-off — not by taste
"Write 5–10 and pick one" is not a procedure. This is the procedure:

1. **Produce 8.**
2. **Binary test:** does it hand the reader **a new belief**? No → cut.
3. Attack the survivors: **saturation** (how many times has this been said this
   month?) · **voice match** (is this you, or someone else?) · **free
   elsewhere?** · **breaks at ×10** (if it goes wide, does the claim still
   hold?).
4. **Kill until one is left, and write down why it won** — that reason is the
   caption.
5. **The killed go on a ladder**, they don't get deleted.

Play **skeptical investor**, not helpful assistant. The difference shows in the
output.

### Chain the slides grammatically
A copy trick, not a design one: make the headlines **continue each other**, so
every headline is incomplete without the one before it.

```text
The photo got them through the door…
and the menu made them hesitate…
and the first bite decided everything.
```

## Slide copy

```text
One sentence, maybe two. More = a second idea = a second slide.
Address them directly: you · do this · don't do this · save this
Specific, not abstract: "Miss two sessions and the third one stops happening"
                        not "consistency matters"
```

**A 1–2 word label + one actionable sentence.** That is the shape of a grid
point.

**The deletion test:** remove a line — did the value drop? No? Then it was
padding.

### Terminology

Keep established technical terms in their original language rather than forcing
a translation; over-translating produces a register nobody speaks.
→ see `references/scripts-rtl.md`

### Banned
- Throat-clearing: "the truth is…" · "let me tell you something" · "here's the thing"
- Adverbs: actually · literally · simply · honestly · genuinely
- Emphasis crutches: "let that sink in" · "and this matters because"
- Generic lines that would fit anyone: "content matters" · "design makes a difference"
- Non-human subjects: "the data says" → **who** said it?
- Lazy generalisations: every · always · never · everyone
- Jargon: let's dive deeper · unpack · game-changer

> ⚠️ **The exception:** the "don't write X, it's Y" ban applies to the body and
> the caption. **The cover is allowed it** — a ceramicist's "It isn't the clay.
> It's the kiln." works, full stop. Same goes for questions, sentence fragments
> and lists of three — those are slide tools.

---

## Red lines

1. **No invented numbers.** Either from real work, or an industry figure
   **attributed to its source**.
2. **Never belittle anyone** — not customers, not named competitors. Mock the
   **situation**, not people.
3. No false urgency, and no promise bigger than what you actually deliver.
4. **Honesty:** if you haven't reached the result yet, the correct phrasing is
   "I'm working toward X and this is the thing that has moved it most" — not
   "I did X."

## The grid slide — the heart of the post

This is the one that gets saved. Without it the post gets read and never
returned to.

- **4–6 points.** Five is the count that repeated most across the reference set.
- Every point: a **short label** (1–2 words, in colour or on a chip) + **one
  actionable sentence**.
- A **fixed vertical step** between points (**~11% of canvas height**).
- If a point has to wrap to two lines, **grow downward** — **never shrink the
  type**.
- Six chips in six colours = the one exception to the single-accent rule,
  because colour here is a **classification system**, not emphasis.

A language school's grid — one label per error type, one sentence on how to fix
it — is the whole post in a single screenshot.

**The summary slide:** collapse everything into **one equation** (`A + B + C`).
That is the slide that gets screenshotted.

## The CTA

**One. Not two.**

```text
one word · easy to automate · repeated 3× in the frame
```

| Type | Shape |
|---|---|
| Comment | "Comment [KEYWORD] and I'll send you [the thing]" ← the strongest: a comment is a ranking signal |
| Save | "Save it — you'll want it back before your next shoot" ← **give it a future moment of use** |
| Share | "Send this to someone who mixes their own records" ← **name the recipient, not the action** |
| DM | "DM me [KEYWORD]" |

**The comment ladder:** open with a broad question and close with **"reply with
one word"** — that last line is what actually converts, because it is the
cheapest possible reply.

**Zero-risk framing:** "a straight answer — even if the answer is that yours is
already fine" breaks the suspicion that it's a trap.

**The CTA slide breaks the template by subtraction:** drop the image, drop the
number, drop the chassis. That sudden subtraction is what brakes the swipe.

## The caption

```text
Line 1 = a hook as strong as the cover   ← this is what shows in the feed
2–3 lines of compressed value
a save/share nudge
the DM keyword
5–10 hashtags
```

**No links in the body** — they cut reach.

**The "friend or funnel" test:** read the caption out loud. If it sounds like a
message from a friend → ship it. If it sounds like a campaign → rewrite it.
Banned: "FREE DOWNLOAD 🚨" · "grab the freebie" · "packed with value" · fake
countdowns. A law firm's caption that reads like a colleague explaining
something at a table beats one that reads like a landing page, every time.

## Diagnosis — before any writing

| Question | Why |
|---|---|
| **The focus** | "I help [who] who feel [the tension] by showing them [the way]" — written once and locked for the brand |
| Who is the audience? | it changes the pain and the vocabulary |
| **Who is this NOT for?** | exclusion defines an audience more sharply than description, and it lets the hook exclude |
| **Awareness stage?** | Unaware / Problem-aware / Solution-aware / Product-aware |
| The pain right now? | the raw material of the hook |
| **What do they want on the surface vs underneath?** | surface = slide 2 (the mirror). underneath = slide 4 (the mechanism). A ceramicist's buyer wants a mug on the surface; underneath they want a house that looks like someone chose it |
| The objection in their head? | one slide has to break it |
| **What are you against?** | this is the fuel for every reframe or counter-expectation hook |
| The evidence available? | from the **evidence inventory** below — not from memory |
| **Funnel stage?** | TOF / MOF / BOF — this sets the ceiling on the CTA |
| The goal? | authority → save/share → DM. **In that order.** |
| The single CTA? | one word, and inside the stage's ceiling |

### The evidence inventory
"No invented numbers" is a ban with no substitute. Build **one list per brand**
holding: real outcomes with their numbers · projects actually delivered ·
clients by name (with their permission) · industry figures **with their
source**. Every number in every post is pulled from that list, or it doesn't get
written.

### Funnel stage — the CTA ceiling
| Stage | The post's job | **Maximum** CTA allowed |
|---|---|---|
| **TOF** | reach people who don't know you | save / share. **Zero product mention.** |
| **MOF** | trust — "this is what I give everyone" | one-word comment → a free thing |
| **BOF** | "what is actually inside X" | one-word DM → the link |

**Don't skip a stage.** A TOF post that asks for a DM burns itself. And the free
thing has to be **page one of the product itself** — same look, same voice — so
that upgrading feels like opening the rest of something they are already
holding. A SaaS team's free audit template should be the exact template inside
the paid onboarding, not a lesser version of it.

> **Where the ideas come from:** [ideas.md](ideas.md) — story mining, gaps,
> signals, and the angle bank.

> **Most audiences are Problem-aware:** they feel the symptom ("nobody's
> booking") and don't know the cause ("your first slide never says what you
> do"). **Start the hook at the symptom.** The post's job is to move them one
> stage forward.

**One angle per post.** Two angles means two posts.

## Delivery shape

Anything this skill produces closes on three things — **not an open list**:

1. **The strongest** — one pick. "They're all good" means no work was done.
2. **What got cut** — and why. If nothing was cut, no editing happened.
3. **What got deferred** — on the ladder, so it doesn't get lost.
