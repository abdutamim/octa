# The Idea Machine — Never Run Out of Content

The idea is not to be a genius — it's to be **organized**. A clear system means your brain stops
tiring from thinking and starts working smart. Use this when someone is stuck for ideas, wants a
brainstorming system, or needs to fill an Idea Bank.

**The origin story to convey:** capture ideas the instant they arrive (Karim used a personal
Instagram group, "KH Scripts," sending each idea with two words on *why* it caught him). After
months → an Idea Bank of 30–40 ideas ready to become scripts anytime. Ideas that aren't captured
*vanish* within a week. So: **build a capture system first.**

---

## Idea selection — 3 filters

Every idea should be:
1. Inside your **Content Pillars**.
2. Carrying real **Value** for people.
3. **Engaging** enough to make the viewer continue.

---

## The 5 idea sources

1. **Competitors** — what's working for people in your niche? How can you re-present it in *your*
   style? (References, not competitors.)
2. **Recurring questions** — every comment/DM question is a ready idea.
   *"How do I start without a camera?" → "The strongest way to start with just your phone."*
3. **Reels that grabbed you** — watch as a *creator*, not a viewer. Log *why* it hooked you: the
   sound? the hook? the idea?
4. **Daily situations** — every work/life moment is a content idea. Tell a story, share a
   frustration or a real win.
5. **The platforms themselves** — Instagram Search / TikTok Studio to see what's Viral in your
   niche.

---

## Fresh angle — same idea, new perspective

"Every idea can be done 5 different ways, depending on the angle you look from." Add a new
**Insight** or **Perspective**:
- A detail no one noticed.
- A viewpoint that flips the conventional take.
- A shocking sentence that changes how people think.

---

## Tools that turn you into an idea machine

- **ChatGPT** — clear prompts, e.g. "give me 30 Reels ideas for freelancers in the marketing
  niche," then keep the dialogue going until ideas are execution-ready.
- **Notion** — store & organize by pillar; board with status (Scripted → Ready → Filmed → Posted).
- **Glasp.ai** — track global trends in your field, import ideas before they hit the Arabic market.
- **ReelSpy** — watch what's viral by the numbers; derive similar ideas for your niche.

---

## Honesty note (important)

Be honest *and* visually smart. If you don't have Proof, say you're *trying to get there*, not
that you've arrived. People respect honesty + real effort more than claims.

---

## Producing an idea bank

**Intake:** niche, audience, pillars (if defined), and their real experiences/stories worth
mining. **Output:** 20–30 ideas/week is the target skill; produce a concrete, niche-specific
batch, each tagged to a pillar and (optionally) an angle/insight. Avoid generic filler — tie each
to a real problem, question, or moment.

**Set up the system, not just the list:** recommend a capture habit (quick group/note the second
an idea lands) and a Notion/Sheet with the status workflow, so they stop relying on "inspiration."
