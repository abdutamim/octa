## 2. Improved Coverage After Getting to Yes

- Principled negotiation: adds a merits-based alternative to will, positional bargaining, and pressure.
- Objective criteria: adds a candidate safeguard for contested price, terms, standards, and fairness claims.
- BATNA: adds no-deal alternative as self-protection, with explicit prohibition against buyer-BATNA weaponization.
- Anti-pressure guardrails: strengthens "yield to principle, not pressure" across scarcity, urgency, closing, tension, and threats.
- Dirty-trick detection: adds detection and resistance categories for deception, psychological warfare, positional pressure, fake authority, fake facts, threats, and lock-ins.
- No-deal as legitimate outcome: adds an explicit ethical counterweight to conversion-only sales logic.
- Buyer autonomy: strengthens self-protection, competing standards, fair procedures, and the buyer's right to say no.
- Fairness / legitimacy: adds the need for genuine, contestable standards rather than seller-owned claims.
- Process negotiation: adds the idea of negotiating the rules of the game when pressure or tricks appear.
