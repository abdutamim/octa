# Brand Building

A complete, practical system for building a brand on social media and producing content plans
that convert. It encodes a full course methodology into staged references you pull from as needed.

**The philosophy (keep it throughout):** start *now* with minimal resources, focus on one clear
niche, and treat content as a *marketing tool* for a real skill or business — not as the goal
itself. You don't need to be the best or know everything; you need to produce a clear result and
be genuinely yourself. Authenticity over perfection. Ship imperfect.

## The journey (9 stages)

Building a brand runs through these stages. A request may touch one stage or the whole arc — route
accordingly, don't force the user through all nine if they only asked for one.

1. **Foundations** — mindset ("you're good enough"), how to learn, result-first thinking.
2. **Niche selection** — pick a smart starting specialty (it's a start, not a life sentence).
3. **Profile optimization** — make the account self-explaining and set up to convert.
4. **Content pillars** — the strategic identity; 5 Pillars + 3C Funnel.
5. **Idea machine** — a system that never runs out of ideas; the Idea Bank.
6. **Scripts** — the 4-stage viral script; credibility.
7. **Hooks** — 12 hook types to stop the scroll.
8. **Production** — film content that looks expensive with just a phone.
9. **Algorithm** — how 2025 algorithms test and reward content, per platform.

## How to route a request

**Step 1 — Identify the stage(s).** Map the request to one or more stages above. Examples:
- "Help me build my personal brand from scratch" / "I want to start but don't know how" →
  **end-to-end**: walk stages 1→9, but pace it — don't dump everything at once.
- "I can't pick a niche" / "should I do X or Y?" → stage 2 (+ stage 1 if they're frozen by fear).
- "Fix my bio / set up my profile" → stage 3.
- "Build me a content plan / content calendar" / "what should I post?" → stage 4 (+5).
- "Give me content ideas" → stage 5.
- "Write me a script / a reel" → stage 6 (+7).
- "Write me a hook" / "my videos don't get views in the first seconds" → stage 7.
- "How do I film this?" / "I don't have a camera" → stage 8.
- "Why is my reach low?" / "how does the algorithm work?" → stage 9.
- "I'm scared to post / the market is saturated" → stage 1.

**Step 2 — Read the relevant reference file(s)** before producing (see the map below). Read more
than one when the request spans stages. Don't answer complex brand questions from memory — the
methodology's specifics (the scoring system, the 12 hooks, the 3C funnel, the signals) live in the
references.

**Step 3 — Intake only what's missing.** Check the conversation first; don't re-ask what you
already know. For anything you still need, prefer **tappable questions via the `ask_user_input`
tool** (easier on mobile) over prose questions. Keep intake tight — ask only what changes the
output. The near-universal intake items: **niche, audience, what they sell/offer, main platform,
goal (reach vs. trust vs. sales), and their voice/angle.** Each reference lists its specific intake.

**Step 4 — Produce**, grounded in the methodology, in the user's language, in the requested format.

## Methodology at a glance (inline gist)

Enough to orient; the references hold the detail.

- **Mindset:** the market never gets full of *real* people. Don't compete — reference. Angle >
  novelty. Every video is a feedback loop, not a verdict.
- **Learning:** micro-learning + apply immediately. You're a specialist with something to sell;
  content is the marketing tool. Learn the *minimum* to produce a result (Deletion Filter).
- **Niche:** it's a *start*, not final. Score options on demand, pricing, growth speed, proof,
  ease of start, future doors — pick the highest. The costliest move is standing still.
- **Profile:** username, picture, name-field (with specialty), bio = mini-pitch ("who I am + who
  I help + on what + CTA"). Highlights = mini-portfolio. First 3 videos define you.
- **Content pillars:** 5 Pillars (Educational Value, Authority & Proof, Personal & Relatable,
  Conversion, Trend-Adaptive) for *type balance*; 3C Funnel (Connecting → Consideration →
  Closing) for the *buyer journey*. Pick 3–5 pillars; build an Idea Bank; keep it balanced.
- **Idea machine:** capture the instant an idea lands or it vanishes. 5 sources: competitors,
  recurring questions, reels that grabbed you, daily situations, the platforms. Same idea × new
  angle.
- **Scripts:** 4 stages — Hook → Build-up → Value/Insight → natural CTA. Optimize for Retention;
  every second earns its place. Credibility > claims (a journey-in-progress beats a fake flex).
- **Hooks:** first 2–3 seconds decide everything. 12 types; pick per personality/niche; make it
  targeted, punchy, specific; A/B test before filming.
- **Production:** you don't need gear — natural light, back camera at 1x, clean frame, good audio
  (audio > video when talking). `Quality = Clarity + Emotion + Simplicity`. 4 video types:
  Sit-Down, Vlogs, Faceless, Screen-Recording.
- **Algorithm:** it's your audience, not your enemy. Micro-test (<1%) → extended → categorize.
  Signals: Retention (top), Scroll-stop, Sound use, Replay, Profile clicks, Comment quality. Each
  platform differs (TikTok = For You + Trend/Raw/Hook; Reels = Saves + Shares; Shorts = first 5s).

## Output modes

The user picks; default sensibly by request.

**In-chat plan** (default for quick asks, ideas, single scripts/hooks, advice): present it cleanly
and directly. Use the user's language. Keep it usable, not academic.

**File** (when they ask to "save," "download," want a template, a calendar, or a formal deliverable):
- **Content plan / pillars / calendar → Excel.** The ready asset is
  `assets/content-pillars-3C-template.xlsx` (3C funnel, sub-pillars, sample ideas, and an empty
  "YOUR IDEAS" column). Copy it to a writable location, fill the "YOUR IDEAS" / CONTENT TYPE /
  CHANNEL(S) columns with their tailored ideas, and present it. For a custom sheet, **follow the
  `xlsx` skill.**
- **Brand strategy / profile write-up / script → Word or Markdown.** For a polished Word
  deliverable **follow the `docx` skill**; for a lightweight doc, Markdown is fine.
- Always save final files to `/mnt/user-data/outputs/` and present them with `present_files`.

When unsure whether they want a file, produce the plan in-chat and offer the file at the end.

## Working style

- **Warm, direct, practical** — the coaching voice of the methodology. Encourage starting now;
  don't let "waiting for the right conditions" survive.
- **Pace end-to-end journeys.** If someone wants the whole thing, guide stage by stage with small
  intakes, not one giant wall. Confirm their niche/positioning before generating downstream assets
  (pillars, ideas, scripts) so everything stays coherent.
- **Ground every deliverable in *their* specifics** — real niche, real audience, real problems.
  Avoid generic filler; that's the difference between a usable plan and a template.
- Leave a **completion prompt** where the references provide one (e.g. "When someone sees my
  content, they must feel I'm the person who ___") to help them internalize positioning.
