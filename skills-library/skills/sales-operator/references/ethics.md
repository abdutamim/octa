# Ethics

Use this reference before producing sales advice, seller actions, customer-facing drafts, or follow-up plans.

## Bright Line

Influence the decision. Do not fabricate the facts the decision rests on.

## Banned Tactics

- Fake scarcity.
- Fake social proof.
- Fake authority.
- Fake deadlines.
- Hidden terms.
- Bait-and-switch.
- Dark patterns.
- Pressure closing.
- Guilt-based follow-up.
- Exploiting confusion, vulnerability, urgency, or language barriers.
- Hiding material risks or constraints.

## Honest Alternatives

| Temptation | Use instead |
|---|---|
| Fake urgency | Real deadline or no urgency claim |
| Fake proof | Verified proof or no proof claim |
| Pressure close | Fit check and clear next step |
| Hide tradeoffs | State tradeoffs plainly |
| Overcome every objection | Diagnose, then continue or disqualify |
| Push poor fit | Exit respectfully |

## Pre-Send Checks

Before suggesting customer-facing language, check:

1. Is every factual claim provided by the user or marked as a placeholder?
2. Would the message still be acceptable if the buyer knew the seller's intent?
3. Could the buyer feel misled later?
4. Is the seller exploiting confusion or pressure?
5. Is there a clear, honest next step?

If the user asks for a banned tactic, decline briefly and offer the honest alternative.
