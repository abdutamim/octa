# Market Map — AI systems + web builds, Egypt & Gulf

Evidence pass: 2026-08-04, three research sweeps (chatbot verticals · AI systems
by vertical · web/e-commerce build demand). Read with `tamim-raddad.md`.
**Everything marked "unverified" must never be quoted to a client.**

---

## The wedge (the strongest single finding)

The market is **siloed**, and the silo is the opportunity:

```
Web shops        sell sites with no brain
Automation shops sell bots with no store
Platforms        RENT automation as a SAR 40–299/mo app that doesn't know the business
```

No vendor was found anywhere in the region publicly selling one integrated
"store + its own AI layer" build at one price. Meanwhile the demand for both
halves is **proven and transacting** — Salla's own App Store lists at least 8
paid AI/WhatsApp apps in live merchant use (Hulul SAR 150/mo, Karam AI 299,
Salla Chat 149, Mersal 99, Mando CX 221), and standalone agencies sell WhatsApp
builds separately at AED 5,000–25,000 setup + AED 500–3,000/mo.

**Position:** the merchant's brain is *owned, not rented*, and it is built by
the same person who built the storefront. This also solves the credibility
problem — 11 shipped stores is not a detour from AI, it is the qualification
for it.

## Where the money actually concentrates (ranked by willingness-to-pay ÷ solo difficulty)

1. **E-invoicing document processing (ZATCA Phase 2 · Egypt ETA).** The only
   *regulatorily forced* spend found — companies buy because the law says so,
   not because AI is exciting. Bounded delivery (Arabic OCR + a known schema).
   Vendors already monetise it (Matix ERP claims 6,000+ MENA clients).
   ⚠️ Compliance risk is on you — a wrong schema means client fines.
2. **n8n / workflow automation builds.** Live job postings right now, including
   one seeking an **Egypt-based** automation engineer; Dubai n8n agencies claim
   30+ regional implementations. No language moat needed, skill transfers
   directly. Best difficulty-to-payoff ratio found. **This is the cash-now track.**
3. **Vertical WhatsApp + store integration** across the ✅ verticals above.
   Same mechanic each time, vocabulary swapped — proven by ~8 independent
   vendors converging on it.

Runners-up with real but unpriced demand: legal contract review (Waddah, Adel,
Adala — Saudi-built, live), internal SOP/RAG assistants (Saudi Digital
Government Authority published an Enterprise RAG policy paper Jan 2026 — a
government-level signal), Arabic call-centre QA.

**Read this as:** ردّاد at AED 6,500 is the *entry* rung, not the ceiling. The

$5 gigs), off-the-shelf Salla/Zid theme swaps (SAR 250–500 ceiling), simple
landing pages, plain auto-reply bots.

## Saudi channel: Salla & Zid

Salla — **60,000–68,000 active merchants**, GMV reported SAR 42.9B; Partners
Portal + App Store + theme marketplace. Zid — ~9,350–10,000 stores, Saudi
stores **+94% YoY** in Q2 2026, Partner Dashboard with explicit revenue-share
on apps, themes and services.

Both are SAR-native and open to outside developers. Two plays:
- **Services now:** custom themes and custom apps for individual merchants.
- **Product later:** list one app on the App Store — 60k+ merchants are a
  built-in distribution channel, and comparable apps sit at SAR 99–299/mo.
  Follow the sequencing rule (`11`): service → productized service → product.
  Do not start here.

Shopify stays relevant for Egypt-based and cross-border merchants (~19% of
Egyptian stores per one source).

## Regional gotchas

- **1 Oct 2026** — Meta begins billing WhatsApp service messages inside the
  24-hour window that are free today. Build usage caps and alerts from day one.
  Per-country Egypt rates conflict across sources ($0.0644 vs $0.1073) —
  verify on developers.facebook.com before quoting.
- **Dialect is the real moat.** Documented: models handle Arabic worse than
  English, MSA-trained models fail on Egyptian/Gulf dialect and on the
  Arabic-English code-switching people actually type on WhatsApp.
- **Data residency:** Saudi PDPL/NDMO pushes sensitive data in-Kingdom; UAE has
  extra health-data rules. In-region cloud exists — factor hosting into quotes
  for clinics and anything government-adjacent.
- **Buying signals:** MENA AI funding $858M in 2025 (22% of all MENA VC); UAE
  AI-related hiring 32%→48% of postings; SAMA and UAE public-sector AI
  mandates. Egypt AI deals: $73M / 15 deals — real but modest.
