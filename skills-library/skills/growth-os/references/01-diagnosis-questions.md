# 01 — Diagnosis

Marketing rarely fails everywhere at once. It fails at **one link** in a chain.
Find the link. Fix only that. Then re-measure.

---

## The chain

```
Product → Market → Persona → Awareness → Offer → Message → Creative
→ Channel → Landing Page → Checkout → Operations → CRM → Retention
```

Walk it in order. The **first** broken link is the only one that matters right
now — fixing anything downstream of it is wasted work.

---

## Symptom → cause tree

| Symptom | Usual real cause | Where to fix |
|---|---|---|
| No views | Attention problem, not a content problem — weak hook or weak format | `06` |
| Views but no followers | No reason to return; no clear identity | `04`, `05` |
| Followers but no leads | Wrong audience, or no CTA / no offer | `02`, `03` |
| Leads but no sales | Weak offer, weak proof, or price mismatch | `02`, `11` |
| Sales but no profit | Unit economics, CAC, or returns | `10` |
| It worked, then stopped | Creative fatigue or audience saturation | `06`, `10` |
| Inconsistent — one hit, then nothing | No system, no repeatable format | `05`, `09` |
| Burnt out, everything depends on me | Level 3, the Beautiful Prison | `12` |

---

## Key distinction

> **"If you have no views, you don't have a content problem — you have an
> attention problem."**

These require completely different fixes. A content problem is solved by better
substance. An attention problem is solved by format and hook (`06`). Most people
misdiagnose this and spend months improving substance nobody ever sees.

---

## Diagnostic questions by link

**Product** — Has anyone paid for this? Has anyone paid twice?
**Market** — Is anyone actively searching for this, or must demand be created?
**Persona** — Can you name one real customer and quote them?
**Awareness** — Which of the 5 stages is the audience actually in?
**Offer** — Would you buy it at this price if you were them?
**Message** — Could a competitor publish your copy unchanged?
**Creative** — What is on screen at second zero?
**Channel** — Is the audience genuinely there, or is it just where you're comfortable?
**Landing page** — Does the first screen match the ad literally?
**Checkout** — How many taps from interest to paid?
**Operations** — What breaks if orders triple tomorrow?
**Retention** — What percentage buy a second time?

---

## Competitor teardown (do not skip)

**Do not reinvent the wheel.** Every serious creator, and every creator's team,
researches what already got views in their space before producing anything.

Pick 3–5 competitors — include at least two **small accounts punching above their
weight**, since their results prove the format works independent of audience size.

For each:
```
Top 5 posts — by saves and shares, not likes
Which format? Which hook type? What is at second zero?
What do they promise?
What do the comments complain about?  ← this is your content brief
What do they never talk about?        ← this is your gap
Offer, price, guarantee
Where they are weak
```

Output: **the one thing we will do differently, and the one format we will copy.**

---

## Output: Diagnosis Report

```markdown
**Symptom:** [what the user sees]
**Real bottleneck:** [one link in the chain]
**Evidence:** [what points to it]
**What we are NOT fixing now:** [and why]
**Expected impact of the fix:** [range, with assumptions]
**First action:** [today]
```

One bottleneck. If you name three, you have not diagnosed — you have listed.
