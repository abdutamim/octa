# 03 — Persona & Awareness

## The market ladder

```
100%  the total market
 30%  actively interested in this category
 10%  have real buying intent right now
  4%  the Power 4% — ready to buy today
```

Most content is written for the 4% and therefore ignored by 96% of the feed.
Most reach is wasted on the 100% with no bridge to the 4%.
The job is to **speak to the 30% while capturing the 4%**.

---

## The 5 awareness stages (Eugene Schwartz)

| Stage | They know | Message must lead with | CTA ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Nothing | A story or symptom they recognize | Follow |
| Problem-aware | They hurt | Name the problem precisely | Save / share |
| Solution-aware | Solutions exist | Why this category of solution wins | Free value |
| Product-aware | They know you | Your mechanism + proof + differentiation | Book / DM |
| Most-aware | They're convinced | Offer, price, guarantee, deadline | Buy |

**Rule:** never ask for a CTA above the stage's ceiling. Asking an unaware
viewer to buy is why conversion looks broken when the real problem is sequencing.

## Dream buyer file

```markdown
### Description
Age · job · city · income · family · daily routine · where they scroll and when

### Psychological map
What they want to be seen as · what they fear being seen as
What they blame for their situation · what they secretly suspect is their fault
What "winning" looks like in their own words

### Buying behaviour
How they research · who they ask · what makes them hesitate
How long the decision takes · who else has a say
Price anchors already in their head

### Need / Want / Demand
Need: the functional requirement
Want: the specific form they want it in
Demand: the version they will actually pay for
```

## Objection bank

Collect every objection, then deploy each one in **three places**:

1. **In content** — a post that answers it before they ask.
2. **In the FAQ** — on the landing page or offer doc.
3. **In the offer** — as a bonus or guarantee that structurally removes it.

The six that recur in almost every market:

```
1. Too expensive
2. No time
3. Won't work for my specific situation
4. I can do it myself / for free
5. I've been burned before
6. Not right now / no urgency
```

---

## Voice-of-customer bank

Harvest **verbatim** language from: DMs, comments, reviews, sales calls,
competitor comment sections, forum threads, support tickets.

**Keep the slang. Keep the grammar mistakes. Keep the exact phrasing.**
Copy written in the customer's own words outperforms polished copy, and it is
the single cheapest source of hooks you will ever have.

Store as: `quote → emotion → stage → usable as (hook / caption / FAQ / bonus)`

---

## Market gap — 3 questions

1. What is everyone in this market saying that nobody believes anymore?
2. What is true that nobody is willing to say out loud?
3. What does the audience complain about in competitors' comments?

The answers are your differentiation and your highest-performing content.
