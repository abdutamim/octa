# Research preset: competitor profile

Run the full Octa 100-source multilingual protocol before making a comparison.
If the brief says “find them”, discover at least 15 candidates first and keep
the candidate evidence before selecting the top 5–8. Never present an
unverified candidate as a confirmed competitor.

## Per-competitor evidence

Capture the site and offer, pricing and packaging, positioning, proof, reviews
in the languages where customers speak, public Meta Ad Library pages, public
social presence, hiring signals, changelog or release activity, marketplaces,
and support or complaint threads. Include both 1–3 star and 5 star reviews
where available. Keep one ledger entry per fetched URL and save page text.

## Output

Use the competitor-profiling template and the 15-step performance-marketer
competitor addendum. Compare promise, audience, mechanism, pricing,
distribution, trust, weaknesses, switching triggers, and whitespace. Cite every
claim with a source number, record contradictions, and separate observed facts
from inference. Write the report in the conversation language and preserve the
complete source ledger.
