# Profile Optimization (Instagram & TikTok, 2025)

In 2025 the social account **is** the website. Algorithms read every detail — name, bio, first
3 videos. The clearer and easier the profile is to understand, the higher the chances of reach
and work. Use this when someone wants their profile set up, audited, or rewritten.

**Goal of a good profile:** a visitor understands *in the first second* who you are, what you do,
why to follow you — and there's a clear CTA that turns a follower into a client.

---

## 1. The self-explaining account — 4 core elements

1. **Username** — readable and searchable. `@AliDesigns` beats `@Ali127_x`.
2. **Profile picture** — professional, clear, well-lit.
3. **Name field** — add your specialty here (it's searchable). Example: `Ali | Video Editor`.
4. **Bio = a mini pitch** — "Who I am + who I help + with what + a clear CTA."

**Bio template:**
> "I'm [your role], I help [audience] reach [result] using [method]. [CTA]"

Example (EN): "I help creators build viral brands using storytelling. 👇"
Example (AR): "بساعد الشباب يعملوا براند شخصي يجيب فلوس من السوشيال ✌️"

Keep it clear and **friendly** — a bio is not a CV.

---

## 2. Highlights — the mini-portfolio (Instagram)

Every new visitor should pass through these. They make the profile look professional and build
trust fast. Covers should be uniform, and the **first** highlight should be "Start Here."

Most useful highlight types:
- Start Here / About Me
- Work / Portfolio
- Testimonials / Reviews
- Services / Offers
- Results
- Tips / Free Value

---

## 3. Platform setup

**TikTok:**
- Switch to a **Business account** (unlocks Analytics + website link).
- Use **keywords** in the Bio and Name field.
- First 3 videos = either Viral or clearly "who I am."

**Instagram:**
- Enable **Creator mode**.
- Use Highlights as a quick guide.
- Delete any post that doesn't fit the brand.

---

## 4. Mistakes to avoid
- ❌ Strange username
- ❌ Empty or generic bio
- ❌ Unclear profile picture
- ❌ Account full of random reshares

---

## Intake for a profile task

Ask (only what you don't already know): their role/specialty, who they help, the result they
deliver, their method/angle, their main platform, and the one action they want visitors to take
(CTA). Then produce: a username suggestion or check, a Name-field line, a bio (in their
language), a Highlights plan, and a short "first 3 videos" recommendation.

**Completion prompt to leave them with:** "If someone lands on my page, they must instantly
understand that I'm the person who ______."
