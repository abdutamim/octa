# 00 — Intake & Brief

**Never start planning before this stage is complete.** A plan built on guesses
is worse than no plan, because it looks credible.

---

## Step 1 — Triage (one question, asked first)

> What exactly are we marketing?

```
A) A person / personal brand
B) A product (physical or digital)
C) A service or agency
D) A local business or place
E) A company / corporate brand
```

The answer changes the whole pipeline weight:
- **A** → heavy on `04`, `05`, `06`, `12`
- **B** → heavy on `02`, `07`, `10`
- **C** → heavy on `02`, `11`
- **D** → heavy on `04`, local content, reviews
- **E** → heavy on `02`, `04`, `10`

---

## Step 2 — The fork question

> **A million followers, or a million dollars? Pick one.**

There is no "both". They are different strategies:

| Goal | Strategy |
|---|---|
| **Followers** | Broad topics, mass appeal, trend velocity, volume, entertainment |
| **Money** | Narrow buyer audience, offer-first, proof content, smaller reach, higher intent |

If they refuse to choose, choose for them based on their stated revenue goal and
say so explicitly.

### The costliest mistake in this field

**The offer must exist and be linked in the bio BEFORE the first post.**

The common story: someone builds huge reach during a platform's cheap-reach
window, has nothing to sell, and by the time the product exists the reach window
has closed. Reach is perishable. An offer is not.

So the first checks are:
```
☐ Is there something to buy today?
☐ Is it linked in the bio today?
☐ If someone DMs "how much?" right now, is there an answer?
```
If any answer is no, **build the offer before building the content plan** — even a
rough v1. Fix it later; do not delay it.

## Step 3 — The 35 questions, in 7 batches

Ask **one batch at a time**. Never dump 35 questions at once. After each batch,
reflect back what you heard in one line before moving on.

### Batch 1 — Identity (5)
1. Name, and one sentence describing what this is.
2. How long has it existed?
3. Country, city, and the audience's language.
4. Who is behind it — solo, partners, team?
5. Why did you start this specifically?

### Batch 2 — Current state (5)
6. Current revenue, even as a range.
7. Existing accounts and follower counts.
8. What have you already tried?
9. What worked, and what failed?
10. What is the single most frustrating thing right now?

### Batch 3 — Goal (5)
11. The one number for the next 30 days.
12. The 6-month goal.
13. Followers or dollars? (from Step 2)
14. What would make this month a success in your own words?
15. What happens if nothing changes?

### Batch 4 — Audience (5)
16. Who exactly buys or follows? Describe one real person.
17. What is their problem, in their words?
18. Where do they spend time online?
19. Why would they choose you over the alternative?
20. What do they say when they say no?

### Batch 5 — Offer & proof (5)
21. What are you selling right now, and at what price?
22. What does the customer get, exactly?
23. Is there a guarantee?
24. What real results can you show?
25. Testimonials, cases, or credentials?

### Batch 6 — Resources (5)
26. Hours per week, honestly.
27. Monthly budget for ads and tools.
28. Can you appear on camera? (yes / no / voice only)
29. Who can help — editor, designer, VA?
30. What tools do you already own?

### Batch 7 — Constraints & taste (5)
31. Anything you refuse to do?
32. Legal, cultural, or platform restrictions?
33. Hard deadlines?
34. Three brands whose look you like, three you dislike — and why.
35. Tone: formal / friendly / bold / calm / funny?

## Step 4 — Reflect back

Before moving to `01`, write:

```markdown
**Here is what I understood:**
[5 lines summarizing the project]

**What I'm assuming, because it wasn't stated:**
[list — each with what changes if the assumption is wrong]

**The biggest risk I see:**
[one sentence]

**Confirm or correct, and I'll start the diagnosis.**
```

---

## If the user will not answer

Do not stall. Proceed with explicit defaults and label them:

```
Defaults applied: 5 hours/week · zero ad budget · Instagram primary ·
first paying customers as the goal · camera-capable.
Tell me which of these is wrong and I'll adjust the plan.
```

A plan with labeled assumptions is useful. A refusal to plan is not.
