# Scripts & Hooks — Writing Content That Goes Viral

In 2025 the **script** is the decisive factor, not the camera or the edit. There's no such thing
as "the video was good but didn't spread" — if the script doesn't grab attention in the first
2–3 seconds, the video dies before it starts, even with an expensive camera and cinematic edit.
Use this when someone wants a script written, a hook written, or their draft improved.

**The algorithm measures behavior, not looks.** It asks: did the person stop or scroll? finish or
leave? rewatch? feel enough value to comment or share? It reads *attention*, not words — and
rewards whoever holds it.

---

## The 4-stage viral script

1. **Hook** (first 2–3 sec) — a shocking question, bold claim, or expectation-breaker.
2. **Build-up** (fast) — set context with no rambling or boring intro.
3. **Value / Insight** — information, experience, or a new angle that makes the viewer think
   "oh — I hadn't seen it that way."
4. **CTA** (natural) — not a sell; a simple act: comment, share, reflect.

---

## Real success signals (optimize the script for these)

- **Attention** — did the first seconds stop the scroll?
- **Retention** — did people finish? (the most important metric)
- **Rewatches** — did anyone watch again?
- **Value per second** — does every second mean something, or is there filler?
- **Engagement drivers** — did the script push people to think, ask, comment?
- **Share-worthiness** — is it worth sending to someone else?

Hashtags don't decide the video's fate — the **script** does.

---

## Credibility (more important than ever)

The biggest mistake: saying something that clearly isn't *you*. Viewers in 2025 are sharp and
sense it fast. Don't open with "I made a million in a month." Instead:
> "I'm *trying* to make $1000... and this is the thing that made the biggest difference for me."

The fix is never lying. It's one of: a personal experience, a journey still in progress, or an
honest admission + real learning. That's what builds long-term trust.

## The Hook — stop the scroll

In 2025 the hook is not a luxury. The first 2–3 seconds decide: the video flies or gets buried.
A good hook does 4 things: **Stops the scroll, raises retention, attracts the right audience, and
clarifies "who + for whom" fast.** If the opening is weak, the algorithm won't push the video —
even if the rest of the script is excellent.

### The 12 hook types (pick what fits the person + niche)

1. **Voice/Text Hook** — a direct line hitting the problem/goal.
   *"If you work Freelance, this video will change your work."*
2. **Visual Hook** — an eye-grabbing shot (Before/After, unexpected frame).
   *A bank screen showing 0 + text: "After 3 months of work?!"*
3. **Motion Hook** — a sudden movement (a "STOP" paper pushed to camera, flipping the camera).
4. **Shock Hook** — a shocking or counter-expectation fact.
   *"Some people with 200 followers earn more than people with a million."*
5. **Question Hook** — a question that stings and pulls.
   *"Why do people watch you... but don't buy from you?"*
6. **Curiosity Hook** — an information gap.
   *"One button — if you turn it on, your work will change."*
7. **Sound Hook** — a trendy/impactful sound or music starting first.
8. **Reaction Hook** — start on a trend scene, then enter with your take.
9. **Story/Skit Hook** — dramatize a real situation from your audience's life.
10. **Reply/Stitch Hook** — reply to a trending video with a stronger point.
11. **Mid-Action Start** — begin from the middle of the event, no intro.
12. **Number/List Hook** — fast, catchy lists.
    *"3 things — if you get them, you'll build a brand that sells from month one."*

### Hook mistakes
- ❌ Generic/rubbery: "This video is for you" — for whom?!
- ❌ Long and boring — the first 3 seconds must *punch*.
- ❌ Not targeted — the person must feel it's about *them*.

### Quick hook formula
> "If you [problem/goal]... you need to see this."
> *"If you deal with difficult clients... this video is for you."*

### Test the hook before filming
- Try the hook as a Story and watch the reaction.
- A/B test: two hook versions for the same content — which pulls more?
- Show it to 2–3 of your audience and ask honestly.
- Post a short with just the hook and check engagement.

### Hook examples by niche
- **Productivity:** "3 habits stealing your day — and the 4th is the worst."
- **Fitness:** "If you train and see no result... the mistake is here."
- **Freelance/Business:** "Your first $1000 doesn't need an audience — it needs one right step."
- **Beauty:** "A 7-minute look that flips your whole day's confidence."
- **Career:** "5 sentences you should never say in an interview — the last is a disaster."

---

## Producing a script

**Intake:** the idea/topic, the niche & audience, the goal (reach/trust/sell), and any real
proof/experience they can honestly use. **Output:** a full script in the 4-stage structure with
2–3 hook options (different types) to A/B test, kept honest and every-second-earning its place.
Match the user's language and voice. If they want it as a file, follow the `docx` skill.

**Completion prompt:** "The first 3 seconds of my next videos will always be about ______."
