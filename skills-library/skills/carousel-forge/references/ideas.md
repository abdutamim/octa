# Where the Idea Comes From

The rest of the skill executes an angle. This file generates one.

**The governing rule: no orphan posts.** Every idea must have a **job** and a
**series** it belongs to. If an idea has no job, don't bin it — shelf it.

---

## 1. The Focus — Written Once, Then Locked

```text
I help [who] who feel [the tension] by showing them [the way].
```

**Tension ≠ pain.** Pain is something they can already say out loud. Tension is
**what they won't type into a search box because they don't have words for it
yet**. "Sales are flat" is pain. "I think I'm doing this right and nobody is
noticing" is tension. **Tension sells; pain gets read.**

It works the same for a product or a place:
- Product: pain = "this keeps breaking." Tension = "I don't trust myself to
  pick the right one."
- Local business: pain = "it's too far." Tension = "I don't know if this place
  is for people like me."
- Creator: pain = "reach is down." Tension = "I've stopped recognising my own
  voice."

Lock the sentence and judge every idea against it: **does this serve the
sentence or not?** If not, it isn't getting made now — however good it is.

---

## 2. Three Intake Sources — Not Three Copies of the Same Thing

### a) Inside your own head (the only source nobody can copy)

Interview, **one question at a time** — not a list. And push for specifics:
**the number, the name, the moment.**

| Ask | Why |
|---|---|
| Do you follow a framework? **And what do you call it?** | Naming is ownership. The same advice with a name becomes yours. |
| A real outcome, with a number? | This is the evidence bank — without it, "no invented numbers" is a ban with no substitute |
| An opinion that breaks the consensus in your field? | Fuel for the counter-expectation hook |
| The story you tell every single time? | If it repeats in conversation, it will work in content |
| The mistake you warn people about? | Your objection slide, ready-made |
| The question you get asked most? | That post is already written |
| **Who is this not for?** | Exclusion defines an audience more sharply than any description |
| **What are you against?** | Every framing hook comes out of here |

Translate the same eight for the account type:
- **Product business:** what's the one build decision you'd defend in public?
  What do returns actually tell you? What does a competitor do that you refuse
  to do?
- **Creator:** what do you make that nobody asked for? What piece of your own
  advice have you reversed? What do people misread about you?
- **Local business:** what does a regular know that a first-timer doesn't? What
  do you say no to? What happens here at 6am that nobody sees?

Close with: **what are the 10 angles this document now makes possible?**

### b) The artifact — the gaps

Take a post (pre-publish or already live) and read it **as the audience, not as
the author**. Produce **exactly 3 questions** — not 5 — in the audience's
voice, each one on a real gap, not "add more detail."

Every question has **three outlets**:
```text
Patch it     → a specific addition inside the same post
Next post    → a written hook
Canned reply → a comment/DM drafted before it's asked  (buys first-hour speed)
```
Then name the **biggest gap** — the one whose absence weakens the post most.

In the carousel, that gap becomes **slide 3 (the objection)** or the very next
post.

### c) Outside — the signal, not the news

Take a raw pile (articles, posts, forums, industry chatter) and filter it.
**The output must be an opinion, not a topic:**

```text
❌ Topic:  "talk about the new update"
✅ Signal: "this update says the platform stopped rewarding X — so if you're doing X, change"
```

Rank them by **proximity to the focus**, not by size of the news. Close with two
mandatory items: **the noise you are ignoring**, and **the fastest win** (the
one that is time-sensitive).

## 3. Story Mining — Five Angles From One Event

Any event you lived through holds more than one post. Run it through these five
slots:

| Angle | What it is |
|---|---|
| **The lesson** | "I learned that…" — the most obvious one, **and the weakest** |
| **Against consensus** | the story breaks a widely held belief in the field |
| **The framework** | the story converts into named steps |
| **The evidence** | the story is itself the proof, with its numbers |
| **The mirror** | "this happened to me" → "this happens to you" |

**Don't force all five.** Take only the ones that genuinely exist. Two strong
beats five padded.

**And never invent a detail.** If a client said "sales went up," don't attach a
number to it. If a supplier said "it ships faster," don't put a day count on it.

**Flag the rare story:** if a story is strong, **don't spend it on a throwaway
post.**

---

## 4. Filtering — By Kill-Off, Not by Taste

"Write 5–10 and pick" is not a procedure. This is the procedure:

1. **Produce 8 candidates.**
2. **Binary test:** does this candidate give the reader **a new belief**? No →
   cut.
3. **Attack the survivors** on named axes:
   - **Saturation** — how many times has this been said this month?
   - **Effort-to-return** — how much work, for what?
   - **Brand match** — is this your voice, or someone else's?
   - **Free elsewhere** — why would they wait for you to say something that
     already exists?
   - **Breaks at ×10** — if this post goes wide, does the claim still hold?
4. **Kill until one is left** — and write **why it won**. That reason is the
   caption.
5. **The killed get shelved, not deleted** — on a ladder for future posts.

Play the role of **investor, not assistant**: "you are skeptical, allergic to
generic statements, and your money is in the game."

---

## 5. After Publishing — A Working Post Generates Five

A post got traction? The loop doesn't close. But **build on exactly what
worked, not on the topic**.

| Axis | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Deeper** | same point, one layer down |
| **The next question** | what the comments are actually asking |
| **The other side** | when this advice is wrong |
| **The how** | if what worked was the **what**, make the **how** |
| **The evidence** | the same claim with a real case |

**The comments are the material.** Paste them in and work from them; don't
guess.

---

## 6. Series — No Orphan Posts

Every idea enters a **named series** (only 2–3 series running at a time). Each
series has exactly one job:

```text
Credibility  → makes them believe you
Reach        → makes them send it to someone
Conversion   → makes them move
```

**Anchor posts:** 2–3 a month, made first, with more effort spent on them than
on anything else. Everything else orbits them.

---

## 7. Output Shape

Any ideation session closes in this shape — **not as an open list**:

```text
Angle          one sentence
Why it works   the mechanism, from the hooks table
Series         its name + its job
Stage          TOF / MOF / BOF
Draft hook     6 words or fewer
```

And close with **three mandatory items**:
- **The strongest** — one only. Not "they're all good."
- **The one being cut** — and why.
- **The one being deferred** — on the ladder, with a date.

> The output ends in a **decision**, not a menu. A list of ten ideas with no
> filtering is deferred work, not finished work.
