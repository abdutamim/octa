## Enemy (the idea we argue against)

**«إحنا بنرد لما نفضى».** And its product form: the template bot that replies
in stiff MSA and makes the buyer feel handled by a machine. We argue that
reply speed is not service quality — it is revenue, and it is measurable.

## Differentiation — the three that survive the "who else could say this" test

1. **Dialect, not MSA.** Egyptian + Gulf dialect and Arabic-English
   code-switching. This is a *documented* weakness of generic platforms, not a
   marketing claim.
2. **Built by someone who also builds the store/site.** Listings are a
   catalog; catalog RAG is the same problem he has solved 11 times.
3. **Custom, not a reseller template.** Competitors resell platforms; he
   builds and owns the stack.

## Offer

- **Promise:** every WhatsApp inquiry answered in under 60 seconds, 24/7, in
  the client's dialect — and only qualified leads reach a human.
- **Mechanism:** RAG over the client's live listings (price, area, delivery
  date, payment plan) + a 4-question qualification flow + handoff rule.
- **Proof:** *none in real estate yet.* Week 1 builds the public demo. Never
  claim a case study that does not exist.
- **Guarantee:** installed and running in 7 days or money back. Deliverable-based
  — never guarantee a lead count he does not control.
- **Bonuses, each killing one objection:** dialect test recording (→"AI can't
  do Egyptian") · monthly usage cap + alert (→"the bill will explode") ·
  human-handoff switch (→"I'll lose control of my clients") · 30-day free
  tuning window (→"it'll answer wrong and embarrass me").

## Evidence base (2026-08-04 research pass)

**Verified:** real-estate AI assistants sell in the Gulf at AED 8,500 setup +
AED 2,800/mo with a documented 71-day ROI case; the Dubai market median is
AED 6,500 + 2,400; WhatsApp is the channel in every case study found;
vertical-specific competitors exist (RevoplyAI, Whispyr, Rawabat for
restaurants); Arabic dialect handling is a documented weakness of generic
platforms; clinics and restaurants are the two runner-up verticals with real
published ROI (88 and 54 days).

**Not verified — do not quote:** any Egypt-specific agency retainer or ROI
case study; fashion/e-commerce regional demand for paid AI assistants (only
global SaaS found — that market looks commoditized by $50/mo Shopify apps);
education, legal and logistics demand.

**Deadline that is real (use it, don't fake it):** Meta starts billing
WhatsApp service messages inside the 24-hour window on **1 Oct 2026** —
messages that are free today. Design usage caps in from day one, and verify
per-country rates on developers.facebook.com before quoting (published
third-party Egypt rates conflict: $0.0644 vs $0.1073).

**Data residency:** Saudi PDPL/NDMO pushes sensitive data to in-Kingdom
hosting; UAE has extra rules for health data (matters if he expands to
clinics). In-region cloud regions exist to satisfy this.
