# 10 — Ads, Funnel & Analytics

## The rule before any spend

**Ads scale what works. They do not fix what is broken.**
An unproven product, a thin margin, or a bad landing page means ads only
accelerate the loss.

---

## The funnel chain

```
Reach → Impressions → Frequency → CTR → Outbound CTR → Landing Page View
→ View Content → Add to Cart → Initiate Checkout → Purchase
→ Confirmation → Delivery → Repeat
```

Diagnose by walking the chain and finding the **first sharp drop**. Fix only that.

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Low CTR | Creative + hook | New angles, not new colors |
| High CTR, low LPV | Page speed or promise mismatch | Match first screen to the ad literally |
| High LPV, low ATC | Offer, price, or proof | Strengthen offer and guarantee |
| High ATC, low purchase | Shipping, payment, cost surprise | Show total cost early, simplify checkout |
| High frequency, falling performance | Creative fatigue | New angle or new audience |
| Sales but no profit | Pricing, cost, or returns | Raise AOV or price |

---

## 12 creative angles

```
1. Problem/solution   2. Before/after      3. Customer testimonial
4. Demo / in use      5. Vs the alternative 6. The mechanism
7. Belief break       8. Natural UGC        9. Behind the scenes
10. Offer / guarantee 11. Audience callout  12. Seasonal
```

**Hook half-life:** every hook dies. Prepare the replacement while the current
one is still winning.
**Test the angle, not the details.** Changing button color is not a test.

---

## Account structure

```
1 campaign, 1 objective
  └─ 1 broad ad set, or 2–3 ad sets with distinct interests
      └─ 3–5 ads from genuinely different angles
+ separate retargeting campaign
  (page visitors + video viewers + engagers)
```

- Do not touch the campaign daily. Learning needs data.
- Kill or scale after a meaningful number of results, not after 6 hours.
- Scale 20–30% every 2–3 days, or duplicate the winner into a new campaign.
- **Judge on CAC and net profit, not ROAS alone** — especially with cash on
  delivery, where confirmation and delivery rates decide the real number.

---

## The math, before spending

```
Unit margin      = price − COGS − shipping − payment fees
Max allowable CAC = unit margin × confirmation/delivery rate − required profit
Break-even ROAS   = 1 ÷ (unit margin ÷ price)
```

If max allowable CAC is below the market cost of a customer, do not run ads.
Raise AOV, raise price, or grow organically instead.

**AOV levers:** bundles · upsell · order bump · free-shipping threshold · subscription.

---

## Measurement (the minimum that works)

```
☐ Pixel / tag installed and firing
☐ 5 events: PageView · ViewContent · AddToCart · InitiateCheckout · Purchase|Lead
☐ Consistent UTMs: source / medium / campaign / content
☐ ONE source of truth chosen (platform vs GA4 vs store) and committed to
☐ Weekly dashboard: spend · results · CAC · net profit
```

Platform and store numbers will disagree. That is normal. Track the **trend**,
not the absolute number.

---

## If the budget is zero

1. Daily content per the 30-day plan — volume is the substitute for spend.
2. Reply to every comment and DM within the first hour.
3. Deliberate outreach: a list of 20–50 fitting people; each message contains a
   real, specific observation plus free value **before** any ask.
4. Collaborations with accounts of similar size.
5. Repost everything to a second platform.
