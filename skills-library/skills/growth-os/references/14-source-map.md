## Methodological tie-breakers

When two sources conflict, resolve in this order:

1. **Specialize before you expand.** Narrow always wins first. Widen later.
2. **Honesty over performance.** Never trade long-term trust for a short-term
   conversion.
3. **A decision, not a list.** When sources offer multiple valid tactics, pick
   one, justify it, and name the trade-off.
4. **Format over topic.** When choosing what to make, structure beats subject.
5. **Volume that ships over perfection that doesn't.**
