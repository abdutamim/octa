# Sales Operator Skill

Status: DRAFT — CANDIDATE ONLY — NOT PRODUCTION APPROVED

## 1. Skill Purpose

This is a general Sales Operator reasoning skill for ethical, diagnostic, buyer-centered sales thinking.

It may help with:

* diagnosing buyer state
* understanding problems and impacts
* structuring discovery
* identifying interests behind positions
* reducing decision risk ethically
* using objective criteria
* preserving buyer autonomy
* identifying manipulation risk
* protecting against pressure tactics

It must NOT be:

* a persuasion manipulation engine
* a closing pressure system
* a script generator
* a live-selling weapon
* a final production-approved skill

## 14. Real Estate Project Briefing — Reference Template (Addendum, HAD-004)

Status: `REFERENCE TEMPLATE — DESCRIPTIVE PROJECT INFORMATION, NOT A PERSUASION TACTIC`

This section is a reusable structure for turning a real estate project's facts into a spoken-language briefing (for audio/voiceover use, e.g. personal-branding content about a project). It does not use urgency, scarcity, authority, or social proof cues. Every bracketed placeholder must be filled with a verified, developer-confirmed fact before recording — never a guess.

For each project, gather the data below, then write the script paragraph in natural spoken sentences (not bullet points — bullets don't read aloud well).

### 14.1 Developer's Name and History

Data to gather: developer's legal/brand name; year founded; total number of completed projects; 2–4 flagship past projects with planned vs. actual delivery dates; awards, certifications, or stock listing; total delivered units or square meters if available.

Script pattern: "[Developer Name] was founded in [Year] and has delivered [Number] projects across [Region]. Notable examples include [Project A], handed over in [Date], and [Project B], handed over in [Date]."

### 14.2 Destination and Location

Data to gather: city/region; name of the broader destination or master development; drive time/distance to key landmarks (airport, downtown, coast, ring road); whether the destination is government-backed or part of a major development plan.

Script pattern: "The project is located in [Destination Name], within [City/Region] — [X] minutes from [Landmark 1] and [Y] minutes from [Landmark 2]."

### 14.3 Area (Exact Location)

Data to gather: exact plot address or nearest major road/intersection; total project land area (sqm, acres, or feddan); neighboring projects or landmarks; what units overlook (sea, lagoon, golf course, green space).

Script pattern: "The project sits on [Plot Size], directly [beside/near] [Exact Location Reference], next to [Neighboring Project/Landmark]. Units overlook [View]."

### 14.4 Facilities and Amenities — Maintenance/Management Services for Villas

Data to gather: full amenities list (clubhouse, pools, gym, security, retail, schools, gardens); which services are specific to villas vs. shared with apartments; who manages ongoing upkeep of villa exteriors and gardens (in-house team vs. named third-party facility management company); service frequency (e.g., weekly landscaping, 24/7 security); whether a homeowners' association fee funds this (cross-reference 14.7).

Script pattern: "Villa owners are supported by [Management Company/In-house Team], who handle [landscaping, pool upkeep, security] on a [frequency] basis, covered under the project's maintenance program."

### 14.5 Types and Prices

Data to gather: unit types offered (studio, apartment, duplex, townhouse, villa); size range per type (sqm); starting price per type; price per sqm; currency.

Script pattern: "[Project Name] offers [Unit Type 1] starting from [Size] at [Price], and [Unit Type 2] starting from [Size] at [Price]."

### 14.6 Delivery Date

Data to gather: exact delivery date or quarter/year; phase breakdown if multi-phase; developer's track record on past delivery dates (cross-reference 14.1).

Script pattern: "Delivery is scheduled for [Quarter/Year]. [If phased: Phase 1 hands over in [Date], with later phases following through [Date].]"

### 14.7 Payment Plan and Maintenance

Data to gather: down payment percentage; installment period and plan options (e.g., 5/7/10-year); maintenance deposit due at delivery (amount or %); annual maintenance fee (amount or %) and what it covers.

Script pattern: "Buyers can reserve a unit with [Down Payment]% down, followed by equal installments over [X] years. A maintenance deposit of [Amount/%] is due at delivery, with an annual maintenance fee of [Amount/%] covering [services]."

### 14.8 Finishing Specs

Data to gather: finishing level (core & shell, semi-finished, fully finished); brand names for kitchen, flooring, sanitaryware, or AC if specified; customization options; finishing warranty period.

Script pattern: "Units are delivered [Finishing Level], featuring [Brand/Material] flooring and [Brand] fittings in the kitchen and bathrooms. [Optional: backed by a [X]-year finishing warranty.]"

### 14.9 Assembly Rule

Combine 14.1–14.8 in order as one continuous spoken narrative, project by project. Do not add urgency ("only 3 units left"), unverifiable superlatives ("the best in Egypt"), or claims not confirmed by the developer — these fall under Section 6 (Blocked Behaviors) and Section 9 (Evidence and Claims Rules) even inside this addendum.
