# Routing Reference

Use this to decide what kind of sales help to provide.

## Stage Routing

| Stage | Primary action | Output to provide |
|---|---|---|
| New lead | Identify source, context, and first response need | Intake checklist |
| Triage | Remove spam, wrong-fit, or incomplete leads | Triage decision |
| Qualification | Check problem, fit, authority, timing, and budget clarity | Pass / hold / disqualify |
| Discovery | Understand current state, desired state, constraints, and decision process | Discovery questions |
| Presentation | Connect recommendation to discovered need | Recommendation structure |
| Objection | Diagnose the real concern before responding | Objection handling plan |
| Negotiation | Prepare alternatives, interests, tradeoffs, and walk-away point | Negotiation plan |
| Closing | Confirm fit, decision readiness, and exact next step | Commitment plan |
| Follow-up | Re-engage with a useful reason and clear next step | Follow-up plan |
| CRM/reporting | Record facts, next step, stage, and outcome | CRM note or report |

## Signal Routing

| Signal | Route | First move |
|---|---|---|
| Ready to proceed | Closing | Confirm fit before closing; do not close a poor-fit buyer just because they are ready. |
| Price concern | Objection or negotiation | Diagnose whether it is value, budget, comparison, or timing. |
| Budget uncertainty | Qualification | Clarify budget range or affordability constraint without pressure. |
| Send me information | Discovery | Treat as possible brush-off or valid request; ask one diagnostic question before sending tailored information. |
| No response | Follow-up | Re-engage with a useful reason; do not spam. |
| Multiple decision-makers | Discovery or negotiation | Identify who decides, who influences, and what each person needs to know. |
| Wrong fit | Disqualification | Be clear and respectful; do not force a mismatch. |

## Clarify Before Acting

Ask one concise question when:

- Stage is unclear.
- Channel changes the advice.
- The user asks for facts that are not provided.
- The seller wants a script but the offer, proof, audience, or constraints are missing.
- The request risks unethical pressure or false claims.
