# Research preset: dream buyer persona

Run the full Octa 100-source multilingual protocol before drafting the persona.
This preset is a research instruction, not a license to invent a customer fact.

## Mandatory source mix

Include customer-language forums and reviews, competitor reviews (1–3 star and
5 star), job posts when the buyer is B2B, support threads, and public social
comments. Prefer first-person customer language. Keep the URL, source number,
language, source type, date, quote, and confidence for every extracted claim.

## Output

Use the 32-field Buyer Persona Sheet from the performance-marketer reference
and the VOC section of customer-research. At minimum cover identity and
context, situation, desired outcome, jobs-to-be-done, pains, triggers,
objections, alternatives, buying committee, decision process, budget and
urgency, trust signals, channels, language, phrases to repeat, phrases to
avoid, and post-purchase success. Each field cites source numbers in the
report. Provide the original customer wording and a bilingual explanation.

Rank themes by frequency × intensity, log contradictions rather than choosing
one side, and list unknowns that need owner input. Write the report in the
conversation language and preserve the complete ledger.
